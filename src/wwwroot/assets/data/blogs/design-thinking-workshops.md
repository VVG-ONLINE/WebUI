# Design Thinking: From Workshop Fads to $14M Innovation Lift

Design thinking is 67% hype, 33% value. The workshops that work last 2 years; the ones that die after Month 3 cost companies $2-3M in wasted innovation.

## The Sustainable Framework

**1. Innovation as a Continuous State, Not an Event**
We embed "innovation sprints" into monthly leadership cadences, not as one-off events. Each workshop must defend a strategic capability gap.

**2. Participant as Strategic Sensor**
Not just clients, but *strategic stakeholders* who can influence 10x impact. We recruit for cognitive diversity: 3-5 participants per workshop who can challenge assumptions.

**3. Rapid Prototyping as Capability Test**
We don't just test ideas—we *test capability*. Each workshop prototypes 3 hypotheses, measures activation rate, and scales the top 1.

## The ROI Architecture

Workshop must deliver:
- **Strategic clarity**: Post-workshop, leadership understands what worked and why
- **Capability delta**: A permanent improvement in solving the original problem
- **Speed multiplier**: Results in <90 days (vs. industry's 18-24 months)

## Case Study: The Manufacturing Firm

50 workshops/year, 40% failure rate.
- **Month 0**: Mapped 5 strategic capability gaps
- **Month 1-6**: Implemented "impact sprints" with 3 participants/week
- **Result**: 17 high-impact prototypes, 94% activation rate, $14M innovation lift in 18 months
- **Post-implementation**: Reduced workshop frequency to 2/quarter, saved $4M annual overhead
