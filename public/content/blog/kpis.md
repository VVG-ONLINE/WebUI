# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Most businesses measure the wrong things. They track activity rather than impact, output rather than outcome. The result is a data-rich, insight-poor organization that struggles to drive meaningful improvement.

## The KPI Problem

Traditional KPI frameworks often suffer from:

- **Vanity Metrics**: Numbers that look good but don't drive decisions.
- **Lagging Indicators**: Measures that tell you what already happened, not what will happen.
- **Siloed Data**: Metrics that fail to connect across business functions.

## Building a Better Framework

An effective KPI system should:

1. **Align with Strategy**: Every metric should trace back to a strategic objective.
2. **Balance Leading & Lagging**: Mix predictive indicators with outcome measures.
3. **Enable Action**: KPIs should drive decisions, not just fill dashboards.

## The Data Funnel Approach

Start broad with awareness metrics, narrow through engagement and conversion, and focus on retention and advocacy at the narrow end. This funnel view ensures you're measuring the complete customer journey, not just isolated touchpoints.
